# Dashboard Requirements

## Executive Dashboard

### KPI 1 – Open Issues
Count of all issues not in Closed state.

### KPI 2 – High/Critical Open Issues
Filter by risk rating.

### KPI 3 – Overdue Issues
Due date before today and state not Closed.

### KPI 4 – Average Closure Time
Average number of days from issue creation to closure.

### KPI 5 – Validation Failure Rate
Failed validations divided by total validations.

## Recommended Charts

- Issues by Risk Rating
- Issues by Category
- Issues by Source
- Issues by Assignment Group
- Issues by Root Cause
- Aging buckets: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days
- Open vs Closed trend

## Management Use

The dashboard should help management identify:

- Risk concentration
- Recurring control weaknesses
- Delayed remediation
- Ownership bottlenecks
- Recurring root causes
